About the Directory

WHO WE ARE.

Lipstix Directory is an editorial guide to the gentlemen's clubs, VIP rooms, and booked entertainers worth your night — built and maintained by the team behind Lipstix Gentlemen's Club.

OUR STORY

Lipstix Gentlemen's Club has run Long Island's premier VIP room since 2021. Over the years, customers kept asking the same question after a great night: "where else is worth going?" This directory is our answer — a running, honest list of the rooms and entertainers we'd actually recommend, in our own city and beyond it.

HOW WE PICK

Every listing is chosen the same way we'd want to be judged: reputation, consistency, and whether the experience matches the reputation. We list clubs and entertainers we'd send a friend to. Paid or affiliate placements, where they exist, are always marked clearly on the listing.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

01

Honest Listings

No pay-to-play rankings. A club or entertainer earns a spot on the list because the experience holds up, not because they bought an ad.

02

Respect, Both Ways

We expect every listed venue to treat performers and guests with respect — and we expect the same from anyone using this directory to plan a night out.

03

Kept Current

Clubs close, hours change, and lineups move. We review listings on a rolling basis and pull anything that no longer meets the bar.
