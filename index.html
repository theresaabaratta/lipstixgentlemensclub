Canada — Ontario

TORONTO.

Toronto's licensing rules have kept the scene small and old-school — most of what's left dates back to Yonge Street's showbar era. Here's what's still worth the cover charge, plus the city's go-to name for booking private entertainers.

NO. 01

The Brass Rail

Yonge Street, Toronto

A Yonge Street fixture since before the pole was standard equipment. Walking distance from U of T and Rosedale, it's the club that regulars bring out-of-towners to see first.

Landmark
NO. 02

Zanzibar Tavern

Yonge Street, Toronto

One of the city's oldest running nightclubs — a former showbar stage turned all-night strip club, and still one of the busiest rooms on the strip.

Est. 1960s
NO. 03

House of Lancaster

Toronto, ON

Running continuously since 1983, House of Lancaster keeps a lower profile than its Yonge Street neighbors but holds a loyal, regular crowd.

Since 1983
